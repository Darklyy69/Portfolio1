Seventeen cars. Two lines. One racing story.

THE RED BULL COLLECTION

From the first title car to a new power-unit era

Each card pairs the supplied artwork with that season’s race drivers, headline performance and one quick fact.

RB6

2010

Vettel · Webber

9 wins · Constructors’ P1

First Red Bull double championship; its blown diffuser helped make it the grid’s benchmark.

RB7

2011

Vettel · Webber

12 wins · Constructors’ P1

A qualifying monster: the team took 18 poles in 19 races and sealed both titles early.

RB8

2012

Vettel · Webber

7 wins · Constructors’ P1

After a rules reset removed the old blown diffuser, Vettel’s late charge still delivered title number three.

RB9

2013

Vettel · Webber

13 wins · Constructors’ P1

Vettel ended the V8 era with nine straight victories—still one of F1’s great winning runs.

RB10

2014

Vettel · Ricciardo

3 wins · Constructors’ P2

Ricciardo scored every non-Mercedes victory of the first turbo-hybrid season.

RB11

2015

Ricciardo · Kvyat

0 wins · Constructors’ P4

Power and reliability problems made this Red Bull’s only winless season between 2009 and 2021.

RB12

2016

Ricciardo · Kvyat · Verstappen

2 wins · Constructors’ P2

Verstappen won on his Red Bull debut in Spain, becoming F1’s youngest Grand Prix winner.

RB13

2017

Ricciardo · Verstappen

3 wins · Constructors’ P3

A difficult start gave way to wins in Azerbaijan, Malaysia and Mexico.

RB14

2018

Ricciardo · Verstappen

4 wins · Constructors’ P3

It won on high-downforce streets and at altitude, then closed Red Bull’s long Renault chapter.

RB15

2019

Verstappen · Gasly · Albon

3 wins · Constructors’ P3

Red Bull’s first Honda-powered car immediately won in Austria, Honda’s first F1 victory since 2006.

RB16

2020

Verstappen · Albon

2 wins · Constructors’ P2

A demanding car, but Verstappen’s dominant Abu Dhabi finale hinted at the breakthrough to come.

RB16B

2021

Verstappen · Pérez

11 wins · Constructors’ P2

Verstappen captured Red Bull’s first Drivers’ title since 2013. The RB17 name was skipped for F1.

RB18

2022

Verstappen · Pérez

17 wins · Constructors’ P1

The first ground-effect Red Bull turned early reliability pain into a dominant championship double.

RB19

2023

Verstappen · Pérez

21 wins · Constructors’ P1

It won 21 of 22 Grands Prix—95.5%—and delivered Red Bull’s first 1–2 in the Drivers’ standings.

RB20

2024

Verstappen · Pérez

9 wins · Constructors’ P3

Verstappen won a fourth straight Drivers’ crown despite a mid-season loss of balance and pace.

RB21

2025

Verstappen · Lawson · Tsunoda

8 wins · Constructors’ P3

A huge late comeback brought six wins in the final nine races; Verstappen missed the title by two points.

RB22

2026

Verstappen · Hadjar

0 wins · 2 podiums · Season ongoing*

Red Bull became a full works manufacturer with its own Red Bull Ford Powertrains DM01 power unit.

Artwork & data note

The supplied images are stylized collector-card art, not official technical documents. In particular, the RB22 art shows legacy Honda branding; the real 2026 RB22 uses the Red Bull Ford Powertrains DM01. *RB22 results are current to 13 July 2026.
